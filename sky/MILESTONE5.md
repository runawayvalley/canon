## What changed vs. milestone 4

**Expression renderer (`render_sky.py`).** Extends the nightmare renderer without changing it:
- **CC11 (expression)** per track, applied live in TinySoundFont and written to the MIDI file.
- **Sustain pedal.** TinySoundFont ignores CC64, so the renderer defers note-offs that fall while the pedal is down to the pedal release. CC64 is still written to the MIDI file for use in a DAW.

**Dynamics (CC11 on the sustained instruments: strings, winds, brass).**
- A section-level curve: Hill/Breeze 110 → Take-off crescendo to 118 → **Flight held back at 106–112** → Clouds 100 → swell into the climax → **climax 127** → Landing 110 → 96.
- Phrase breathing: ±2.5 CC around that curve over every 2-bar cycle (a slight swell to the middle of each phrase).
- Harp, piano, celesta, glockenspiel, timpani and cymbal stay at full expression and use velocity only.

**Piano pedal.** Re-pedalled on every beat (the harmony changes every beat), pedal held through the final chord. The left hand's old note-overlap workaround is trimmed now that the pedal provides the sustain.
