## Density pass (after first listen)

The first draft felt rushed: too many notes in a short time, not only the tempo. Changes:
- **Tempo:** 68–74 → 58–62 BPM (piano), 76 → 64 BPM (canon study).
- **Piano:** left-hand dyads every 2 beats instead of every beat; Theme A in half notes instead of quarters; dropped the 3rds.
- **Canon study:** pad holds one chord per 2 beats instead of re-striking every beat; contrabass plays every other ground note (the cello keeps the full ground); V3 and the flute line rewritten with long notes.
- Voicings on beats 2.1 and 2.3 changed (G add9, G6/9) to avoid F#–G rubs against the held pad.

| Piano sketch | 3.5 → **2.3** |
